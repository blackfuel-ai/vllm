**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What's the situation? Why are we deciding anything? What forces are in play (technical, organizational, time)?

Keep this concrete. Reference specific code paths, repos, prior commits where relevant.

## Decision

What did we decide to do? State it plainly, in one or two sentences. Then unpack the mechanics.

## Alternatives considered

What else did we look at? Why did we reject each?

This section is what makes an ADR worth writing — without it, the ADR is just a description. The trade-offs are the value.

## Consequences

What does this decision cost us? What does it buy us? What follow-on work does it imply?

Both positive and negative consequences belong here. Future readers should understand the deal we made.
